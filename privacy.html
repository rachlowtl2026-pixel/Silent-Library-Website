What we collect

When you join the library or contact us, we may collect your name, contact details and borrowing information. We collect only what we need to run our library service.

How we use it

Your details help us manage memberships, loans, reservations and event registrations. We may send essential account messages and, if you opt in, occasional library news.

Keeping it safe

We use reasonable organisational and technical measures to protect personal information. We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

Your choices

You may ask to review or update your details, change communication preferences, or ask questions about this policy at hello@silentlibrary.sg.
