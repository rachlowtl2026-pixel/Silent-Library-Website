About the book

Play is never just a game.

On a winter day in the early 1990s, two childhood friends reconnect in a hospital. Together they create a blockbuster video game, finding fame, joy, tragedy and a way back to each other over three decades.

This is a tender, expansive story about collaboration and ambition — and about the many ways we tell one another, “I love you.”
